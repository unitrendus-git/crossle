How to play

  • Solve every hidden word in the grid before your guesses run out.
  • Tap a word to select it, type a real 5-letter word, and press Enter.
  • Colors describe that word only:
r right spot a in the word, wrong spot t not in this word
  • Green letters lock into the grid, so they also fill in any word that crosses there.
  • Your guess budget is shared across the whole grid. Choosing which word to attack next is the game.
  • Daily is the same grid for everyone each day. Endless deals a fresh grid every time.

Play a friend's grid: enter the code they sent you.
